FAQ

Clear answers before users commit.

No. Earnings depend on approved advertising activity and verifiable views.

Can earners contact merchants?

No. Merchant contact details stay private and the platform acts as the intermediary.

When do ads expire?

About Us

AmplifyNet.co is a Kenyan marketplace built to help businesses distribute approved product campaigns through registered WhatsApp Status earners while keeping payments, verification, and privacy under one accountable platform.

Our Mission

We connect merchants with compliant, measurable status advertising opportunities through verified submissions, configurable commission rules, documented payment records, and clear boundaries between merchants and earners.
